RE Pro Dashboard is the R/E Pro Photos platform for booking and managing real estate photography shoots. Photographers use it to view assignments, appointment details, and their shoot schedule.

Open dashboard

Google Calendar connection is optional.

Google Calendar integration

Keep appointments up to date.

Connect a Google account from Availability in the dashboard. After you grant permission, your assigned shoots sync to the connected Google calendar, with appointment times, property locations, and relevant shoot details.

01 / CREATE

See new assignments

Scheduled shoots and separately scheduled services become calendar appointments, with a link back to the shoot in RE Pro Dashboard.

02 / UPDATE

Follow schedule changes

Changes in the dashboard update the existing calendar event. Cancelled shoots remain visible with a cancellation label.

03 / REMOVE

Keep assignments current

When an assignment is removed or a shoot no longer qualifies for sync, the corresponding event is removed from the connected calendar.

You choose to connect.

The dashboard asks for Google Calendar permission only when you start the connection. Google shows the requested permissions before you authorize access. Your connected account email appears in the dashboard so you can identify the linked calendar account.

Shoot changes flow from RE Pro Dashboard to Google Calendar. This integration does not import unrelated personal calendar events into the dashboard.

Understand your data.

Your assigned-shoot details are sent to the connected Google calendar. These details may include property addresses, client contact information, services, and relevant access or shoot notes.

Read our Privacy Policy for data handling and your choices. You can revoke access through your Google Account connections. For support or a privacy request, email contact@reprophotos.com.
