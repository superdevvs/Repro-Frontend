Upload your shoot media
Here's how to upload your photos and video to a shoot in REPro Dashboard, and send everything to the editing team.

Open the right shoot
From your photographer dashboard, open the assigned shoot. Check the property address, then find Raw Uploads. Choose Upload Files, or Upload More if files are already attached.

Add photos and RAW files
Drag in your images or RAW files, or select them from your computer. Match them to the correct booked service. Only services assigned to you are available.

Check service, brackets and notes
For bracketed photography, check the Bracket setting matches what you captured. Check the selected count, and add any instructions in Notes for Editor.

Upload and wait for completion
Choose Confirm and upload, or Upload all for a batch across services. Keep this browser tab open and wait for Upload complete.

Add video and other booked media
To add video, choose Upload More, select your video clips, and check the video service. Upload them and wait for completion. Use the matching service for drone and other booked media.

Check every file arrived
Review the uploaded files and counts. If a file failed, retry it and confirm it appears before continuing. If a file is rejected, read the upload result and fix the issue before retrying.

Submit to the editing team
When everything is attached, choose Submit Raw Files. Check the confirmation, then select Yes, submit. This sets the shoot status to Uploaded and notifies the editing team.

Upload. Review. Submit.
You're done. Upload first, review every file, then submit. You can replay this guide from Upload guide on your photographer dashboard.
